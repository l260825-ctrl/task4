10/05/25: Trip to Murree

Last weekend we went to Murree with my family. The weather was cold and the hills were covered in clouds. We drank hot tea and walked on the Mall Road until late evening.

10/12/25: Day in Lahore

Today I visited the Badshahi Mosque and then ate at Food Street. The food was amazing and the old city looked beautiful at night. I want to visit again soon with my friends.
